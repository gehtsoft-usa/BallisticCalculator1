static public method BarrelTwist.RecommendedTwist

Brief

Returns the barrel twist (step, distance per turn) needed to reach the target gyroscopic stability.

Declaration
C#
Measurement<DistanceUnit>  RecommendedTwist (Ammunition ammunition, double targetStability, Atmosphere atmosphere = null)

Parameters
ammunition

The projectile. Bullet diameter and length are required.
targetStability

The desired gyroscopic stability factor. Must be greater than 0.
atmosphere

The atmosphere for the temperature/pressure correction. Null means ICAO standard.

Details

This is the inverse of BarrelTwist.Stability : feeding the result back into it reproduces the target.
