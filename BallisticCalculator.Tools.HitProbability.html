class HitProbability

Parents

object

Brief

Estimates the hit probability against a target by Monte Carlo over the shooter's error budget.

Details

Each simulated shot perturbs muzzle velocity, range estimate, wind estimate and aim (the shooter's group), then lands where the (mis-)dialed hold puts it relative to the target center. Nominal (zero-error) conditions land dead center, so the tool assumes the come-up and wind hold are correct for the estimated range and wind. Muzzle velocity and wind enter through the trajectory's sensitivities and range through the actual drop curve, all precomputed from a few trajectory runs.

Static Public Methods

Estimate

Estimates hit probability for the given shot and error budget. The target is at the shot's maximum distance.
