public method TrajectoryCalculator.CalculateZeroParameters

Brief

Calculates the vertical and horizontal barrel adjustments that zero the rifle at the zero distance.

Declaration
C#
ZeroCalculatedParameters  CalculateZeroParameters (Ammunition ammunition, Atmosphere atmosphere, Rifle rifle, ZeroingParameters zero, ShotParameters shot = null, Wind [] wind = null, DragTable dragTable = null, Measurement<DistanceUnit>? accuracy = null)

Parameters
ammunition

Ammunition (overridden by the zeroing ammunition when set).
atmosphere

Atmosphere (overridden by the zeroing atmosphere when set).
rifle

The rifle (sight height and rifling).
zero

The zeroing parameters (distance and impact offsets).
shot

Optional shot parameters; only the shot angle, azimuth and latitude are used.
wind

Optional wind at zeroing; when not set, zeroing is done in calm air.
dragTable

Custom drag table (required when the ballistic coefficient table is GC).
accuracy

Solve accuracy; default 0.1 mm.

Details

The impact is placed on the aim point, offset by the zeroing VerticalOffset and HorizontalOffset when set. This drives the full Calculate trajectory, so spin drift, Coriolis and aerodynamic jump are all accounted for in the zero.

Wind is optional: pass wind to zero under a known condition, folding in its deflection and crosswind aerodynamic jump, or omit it to zero in calm air. The optional shot contributes only its shot angle, azimuth and latitude.
