public property ShotParameters.BarrelAzimuth

Brief

The azimuth of the shot.

Declaration
C#
Measurement<AngularUnit>?  BarrelAzimuth public get; public set;

Details

Compass bearing of the line of fire (0 degrees is North, clockwise to 90 degrees East). Used to orient the Coriolis and Eötvös deflection; it does not tilt the trajectory itself.
