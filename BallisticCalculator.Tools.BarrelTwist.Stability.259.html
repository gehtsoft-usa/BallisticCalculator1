static public method BarrelTwist.Stability

Brief

Returns the gyroscopic (Miller) stability factor for the projectile fired from a barrel with the given twist.

Declaration
C#
double  Stability (Ammunition ammunition, Measurement<DistanceUnit> riflingStep, Atmosphere atmosphere = null)

Parameters
ammunition

The projectile. Bullet diameter and length are required.
riflingStep

The barrel twist as a step (distance per turn), for example 1 turn in 8 inches.
atmosphere

The atmosphere for the temperature/pressure correction. Null means ICAO standard.

Details

Requires the bullet diameter and length. When the atmosphere is null the temperature/pressure correction is 1 (ICAO standard, 29.92 inHg / 59 F).
