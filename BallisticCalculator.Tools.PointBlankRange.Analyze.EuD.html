static public method PointBlankRange.Analyze

Brief

Analyzes a trajectory for its point-blank corridor against the given vital zone size and aim.

Declaration
C#
BallisticCalculator.Tools.PointBlankRangeResult  Analyze (TrajectoryPoint [] trajectory, Measurement<DistanceUnit> vitalZoneSize, BallisticCalculator.Tools.PointBlankAim aim = PointBlankAim.Center)

Parameters
trajectory

The computed trajectory (as returned by the calculator).
vitalZoneSize

The full vital zone height.
aim

Where the line of sight sits within the vital zone.

Details

The vital zone size is the full target height. With a center aim the corridor is half the zone above and below the line of sight; with a bottom aim it is the whole zone above the line of sight. The trajectory must extend past the corridor so the far edge can be found, and all ranges are reported at the resolution of the trajectory's output points.
