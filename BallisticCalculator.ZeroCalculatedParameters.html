class ZeroCalculatedParameters

Parents

object

Brief

The barrel adjustments produced by a zeroing calculation.

Details

Carries the vertical (ZeroDropAdjustment) and horizontal (ZeroWindageAdjustment) angles the sight is zeroed at, so they can be transferred onto a ShotParameters by its Apply method.

Public Properties

ZeroDropAdjustment

Vertical barrel elevation established by zeroing.

ZeroWindageAdjustment

Horizontal barrel angle established by zeroing.
