enum ReticleLineStyle

Parents

Enum

Brief

The style of a line stroke used to draw a reticle element.

Details

Applies to ReticleLine , ReticleRectangle , ReticleCircle and ReticlePath .

Static Public Fields

Dashed

A line drawn as a sequence of dashes.

Dotted

A line drawn as a sequence of dots.

Solid

A continuous, solid line. This is the default when no style is specified.
