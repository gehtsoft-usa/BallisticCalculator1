Brief

A recommended range of barrel twists for a projectile, from the slowest twist that still

Details

reaches a minimum gyroscopic stability to the fastest twist at an over-stable ceiling.

Twist is a barrel step (distance per turn), so a slower twist is a longer step and a faster twist is a shorter step. Higher stability needs a faster twist, therefore MinimumTwist >= OptimalTwist >= MaximumTwist as step lengths.
