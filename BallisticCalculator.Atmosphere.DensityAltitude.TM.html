public property Atmosphere.DensityAltitude

Brief

The altitude in the standard atmosphere which has the same air density as this atmosphere.

Declaration
C#
Measurement<DistanceUnit>  DensityAltitude public get;

Details

The density altitude summarizes into one number everything the air does to the drag, so two sets of conditions with the same density altitude produce the same trajectory. Air that is hot, high or humid raises it above the true altitude, dense air lowers it, and at sea level in conditions denser than standard it is negative.

The value is derived from Density, so it accounts for the humidity as well as for the pressure and the temperature.
